Total Lunar Eclipse to Turn Moon Red

  • 0:00A total lunar eclipse is about to make the moon appear a reddish color across the Western Hemisphere.

    一场月全食即将使西半球的月亮呈现红色。

  • 0:11The event will happen Thursday night into Friday morning.

    这一事件将在周四晚至周五早上发生。

  • 0:18The best places to see the eclipse will be in North America and South America.

    观赏月食的最佳地点将是北美洲和南美洲。

  • 0:28Parts of Africa and Europe may also get brief views.

    非洲和欧洲部分地区也可能短暂看到。

  • 0:34A lunar eclipse happens when the sun, Earth, and moon line up just right, with Earth positioned between the sun and moon.

    当太阳、地球和月球完美排列，地球位于太阳和月球之间时，就会发生月食。

  • 0:49This causes the Earth to create a shadow on the moon.

    这导致地球在月球上投下阴影。

  • 0:55In a total lunar eclipse, the Earth's shadow covers all of the moon.

    在月全食中，地球的阴影覆盖了整个月球。

  • 1:02Another kind of eclipse is a solar eclipse.

    另一种食是日食。

  • 1:07In a solar eclipse, the moon gets in a position where it blocks light from the sun, causing a partial or full shadow on Earth.

    在日食中，月球处于阻挡太阳光的位置，导致地球出现部分或全部阴影。

  • 1:21A total lunar eclipse can also be called a blood moon.

    月全食也可以被称为血月。

  • 1:28It makes the moon appear reddish-orange, similar to the element copper.

    它使月亮呈现红橙色，类似于铜元素。

  • 1:35The color comes from small amounts of sunlight passing through the Earth's atmosphere.

    这种颜色来自于少量阳光穿过地球大气层。

  • 1:43The American space agency NASA says lunar and solar eclipses happen between four and seven times a year.

    美国航天局NASA表示，月食和日食每年发生四到七次。

  • 1:56The last total lunar eclipse was in 2022.

    上一次月全食发生在2022年。

  • 2:01This one will be visible for about one hour starting Friday morning at 2:26 a.m. Eastern Daylight Time (EDT).

    这次月全食将在周五凌晨2点26分（美国东部夏令时间）开始，持续约一小时。

  • 2:16The time when the Earth's shadow covers all of the moon will be close to 3 a.m. EDT.

    地球阴影完全覆盖月球的时刻将接近美国东部夏令时间凌晨3点。

  • 2:25"As long as the sky is clear, you should be able to see it," Shannon Schmoll told the Associated Press.

    “只要天空晴朗，你应该能看到它，”香农·施莫尔告诉美联社。

  • 2:36She is the director of Abrams Planetarium at Michigan State University.

    她是密歇根州立大学艾布拉姆斯天文馆的馆长。

  • 2:43No special equipment will be needed to see the reddish moon.

    观看红色的月亮不需要特殊设备。

  • 2:49The total lunar eclipse may be harder to see in Europe and Africa because the moon will be close to setting.

    由于月亮即将落下，欧洲和非洲可能更难看到月全食。

  • 3:02Michael Faison is an astronomy expert from Yale University.

    迈克尔·费森是耶鲁大学的天文学专家。

  • 3:09He told the AP, "This is really an eclipse for North and South America."

    他告诉美联社：“这确实是北美洲和南美洲的月食。”

  • 3:17Zoe Ortiz is a historian with the University of North Texas.

    佐伊·奥尔蒂斯是北德克萨斯大学的历史学家。

  • 3:24She noted that different civilizations have observed lunar eclipses for thousands of years.

    她指出，不同文明已经观测月食数千年了。

  • 3:34This helped ancient people learn things about the behaviors of the sun, moon, and stars.

    这帮助古人了解了太阳、月球和恒星的行为。

  • 3:43"They were looking at the night sky and they had a much brighter vision than we do today," Ortiz said.

    “他们仰望夜空，看到的景象比我们今天要明亮得多，”奥尔蒂斯说。

  • 3:53The ancient Greek thinker and writer Aristotle observed that Earths' shadow on the moon during a lunar eclipse was always curved.

    古希腊思想家、作家亚里士多德观察到，月食期间地球在月球上的阴影总是弯曲的。

  • 4:07This fact supported proof that the Earth is round.

    这一事实支持了地球是球形的证据。

  • 4:14The next total lunar eclipse will appear in the sky September 7, across parts of Asia, Africa, Australia and Europe.

    下一次月全食将于9月7日出现在天空，覆盖亚洲、非洲、澳大利亚和欧洲部分地区。

  • 4:27Parts of the Americas will get the next chance to see one in March 2026.

    美洲部分地区将在2026年3月获得下一次观测机会。
