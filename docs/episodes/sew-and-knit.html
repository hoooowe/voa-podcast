Sew and Knit

  • 0:00This week on Ask a Teacher, we answer a question from Rohullah in Afghanistan about using the words "sew" and "knit" and related terms.

    本周在《问老师》节目中，我们回答来自阿富汗的Rohullah提出的关于使用“缝”和“织”及相关词汇的问题。

  • 0:16Dear VOA Learning English,

    亲爱的VOA学英语：

  • 0:19I am sending this email to know what the differences are between "sewing and knitting" or "sew and knit."

    我发这封邮件是想知道“缝纫和编织”或“缝和织”之间的区别。

  • 0:30Besides [that], I wanted to know what I can call a woman or a man that makes clothes.

    除此之外，我还想知道可以怎么称呼做衣服的男人或女人。

  • 0:38Thank you

    谢谢。

  • 0:39I'm happy to answer this question, Rohullah.

    Rohullah，我很高兴回答这个问题。

  • 0:43An important difference between sewing and knitting is that when we sew, we connect pieces of fabric or cloth that are already made.

    缝纫和编织的一个重要区别是，当我们缝纫时，我们连接已经做好的布料或织物片。

  • 0:57When we knit, we make the cloth itself from strings called yarn.

    当我们编织时，我们用叫做纱线的线来制作布料本身。

  • 1:04Also, the word "sewing" can describe many ways people connect or repair fabric and clothes.

    此外，“缝纫”这个词可以描述人们连接或修补布料和衣服的多种方式。

  • 1:14For example, people can sew many kinds of clothes, such as socks, shirts, dresses, hats, and gloves.

    例如，人们可以缝制多种衣服，如袜子、衬衫、连衣裙、帽子和手套。

  • 1:26People can also sew larger things, such as window coverings or tents.

    人们还可以缝制更大的物品，如窗帘或帐篷。

  • 1:33A person can sew using a sewing machine or simply by using their hands with a needle and thread.

    一个人可以用缝纫机缝纫，也可以只用针线手工缝纫。

  • 1:43Knitting does not generally require use of a machine or a needle and thread.

    编织通常不需要使用机器或针线。

  • 1:51To knit, a person uses thicker strings called yarn, along with two thin sticks called knitting needles.

    编织时，人们使用较粗的线（称为纱线），以及两根细棒（称为织针）。

  • 2:02Yarn is usually made of small, natural pieces, called fibers, such as wool or cotton.

    纱线通常由细小的天然纤维制成，如羊毛或棉花。

  • 2:12While sewing thread is often less than one millimeter thick, knitting yarn is generally two to five millimeters thick.

    缝纫线通常不到一毫米粗，而编织纱线通常有两到五毫米粗。

  • 2:24Many people who knit make clothing for cold weather, such as wool sweaters, hats, and scarves.

    许多编织者制作寒冷天气穿的衣服，如羊毛衫、帽子和围巾。

  • 2:33They may also knit blankets or other things that can be used in the home.

    他们也可能编织毯子或其他家中可用的物品。

  • 2:40More than 50 years ago, most Americans who earned money sewing clothes in a factory were women. They were called "seamstresses."

    五十多年前，大多数在工厂靠缝制衣服赚钱的美国人都是女性。她们被称为“女裁缝”。

  • 2:53Today, we sometimes call any person who sews a "sewist."

    今天，我们有时称任何缝纫的人为“缝纫者”。

  • 2:60Or we can simply say "a person who sews."

    或者我们可以简单地说“一个缝纫的人”。

  • 3:05And a person whose job is to sew or repair clothes to make them fit well is called a tailor.

    而一个以缝制或修补衣服使其合身为职业的人被称为裁缝。

  • 3:14For our readers and listeners, what are your questions about American English?

    对于我们的读者和听众，你们对美国英语有什么问题吗？

  • 3:21Do you like to knit or sew?

    你喜欢编织还是缝纫？

    我们想听到你的声音！请给我们发邮件至learningenglish@voanews.com。

  • 3:34And please let us know where you are from, too.

    也请告诉我们你来自哪里。

  • 3:39And that's Ask a Teacher.

    这就是《问老师》节目。

    我是安德鲁·史密斯。
