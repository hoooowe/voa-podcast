Wilbur and Orville Wright: The First Airplane

  • 0:00Wilbur and Orville Wright are the American inventors who made a small engine-powered flying machine.

    威尔伯·莱特和奥维尔·莱特是发明了小型发动机驱动飞行器的美国发明家。

  • 0:10They proved that flight without the aid of gas-filled balloons was possible.

    他们证明了无需充气气球就能实现飞行。

  • 0:18Wilbur Wright was born in 1867 near Melville, Indiana.

    威尔伯·莱特于1867年出生在印第安纳州梅尔维尔附近。

  • 0:25His brother Orville was born four years later in Dayton, Ohio.

    他的弟弟奥维尔四年后出生在俄亥俄州代顿市。

  • 0:32As they grew up, the Wright brothers experimented with mechanical things.

    成长过程中，莱特兄弟对机械装置进行了各种实验。

  • 0:39Later, the Wright brothers began to design their own flying machine.

    后来，莱特兄弟开始设计自己的飞行器。

  • 0:45They used ideas they had developed from earlier experiments with a toy helicopter, kites, the printing machine and bicycles.

    他们利用了早期从玩具直升机、风筝、印刷机和自行车实验中得出的想法。

  • 0:58Soon, they needed a place to test their ideas about flight.

    很快，他们需要一个地方来测试关于飞行的想法。

  • 1:04The best place with the best wind conditions seemed to be a piece of sandy land in North Carolina along the coast of the Atlantic Ocean.

    风况最好的地方似乎是北卡罗来纳州大西洋沿岸的一片沙地。

  • 1:18It was called Kill Devil Hill, near the town of Kitty Hawk.

    那里被称为 Kill Devil Hill，靠近基蒂霍克镇。

  • 1:23The Wright brothers did many tests with gliders at Kitty Hawk.

    莱特兄弟在基蒂霍克用滑翔机进行了多次测试。

  • 1:29With these tests, they learned how to solve many problems.

    通过这些测试，他们学会了如何解决许多问题。

  • 1:35By the autumn of 1903, Wilbur and Orville had designed and built an airplane powered by a gasoline engine.

    到1903年秋天，威尔伯和奥维尔已经设计并制造了一架由汽油发动机驱动的飞机。

  • 1:46The plane had wings 12 meters across.

    这架飞机的翼展为12米。

  • 1:51It weighed about 340 kilograms, including the pilot.

    包括飞行员在内，它重约340公斤。

  • 1:57On December 17th, 1903, they made the world's first flight in a machine that was heavier than air and powered by an engine.

    1903年12月17日，他们完成了世界上首次重于空气且由发动机驱动的飞行。

  • 2:11Orville flew the plane 36 meters.

    奥维尔驾驶飞机飞行了36米。

  • 2:15He was in the air for 12 seconds.

    他在空中停留了12秒。

  • 2:19The two brothers made three more flights that day.

    兄弟俩那天又进行了三次飞行。

  • 2:23Four other men watched the Wright brothers' first flights.

    有四个人观看了莱特兄弟的首次飞行。

  • 2:28One of the men took pictures.

    其中一人拍了照片。

  • 2:31Few newspapers, however, noted the event.

    然而，很少有报纸报道这一事件。

  • 2:36It was almost five years before the Wright brothers became famous.

    将近五年后，莱特兄弟才出名。

  • 2:42In 1908, Wilbur went to France. He gave demonstration flights at heights of 90 meters.

    1908年，威尔伯去了法国。他在90米的高度进行了演示飞行。

  • 2:53A French company agreed to begin making the Wright brothers' flying machine.

    一家法国公司同意开始生产莱特兄弟的飞行器。

  • 2:59Orville made successful flights in the United States at the time Wilbur was in France.

    在威尔伯在法国期间，奥维尔在美国成功完成了飞行。

  • 3:07The United States War Department agreed to buy a Wright brothers' plane.

    美国战争部同意购买一架莱特兄弟的飞机。

  • 3:14Wilbur and Orville suddenly became world heroes.

    威尔伯和奥维尔突然成了世界英雄。

  • 3:19But the brothers were not seeking fame.

    但兄弟俩并不追求名声。

  • 3:22They returned to Dayton where they continued to improve their airplanes.

    他们回到代顿，继续改进他们的飞机。

  • 3:29They taught many others how to fly.

    他们教了很多人如何飞行。

  • 3:32Wilbur Wright died of typhoid fever in 1912.

    威尔伯·莱特于1912年因伤寒去世。

  • 3:38Orville Wright continued designing and inventing until he died many years later, in 1948.

    奥维尔·莱特继续设计和发明，直到多年后的1948年去世。

  • 3:48Today, the Wright brothers' first airplane is in the Air and Space Museum in Washington, D.C.

    如今，莱特兄弟的第一架飞机保存在华盛顿特区的航空航天博物馆。

  • 3:57Visitors to the museum can look at the Wright brothers' small plane.

    参观博物馆的游客可以看到莱特兄弟的小飞机。

  • 4:04Then they can walk to another area and see space vehicles and a rock collected from the moon.

    然后他们可以走到另一个区域，看到太空飞行器和一块从月球采集的岩石。

  • 4:13The world has changed a lot since Wilbur and Orville Wright began the modern age of flight over one hundred years ago.

    自从威尔伯·莱特和奥维尔·莱特在一百多年前开创了现代飞行时代以来，世界发生了巨大变化。

  • 4:24I'm John Russell.

    我是约翰·拉塞尔。
