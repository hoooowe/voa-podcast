How Daylight Savings Time Affects Health

Mar 07, 2025 · Health and Lifestyle

▶ VOA Original Audio

English & 中文（点击句子跳转音频）

Click any sentence to jump to that point in the audio. The current sentence highlights as it plays.

  • 0:00Much of the United States "springs forward" on Sunday, March 9th, for daylight saving time.

    美国大部分地区将于3月9日周日“提前”进入夏令时。

  • 0:10Worldwide, many other countries also observe daylight saving time, starting and ending on different dates.

    在全球范围内，许多其他国家也实行夏令时，但开始和结束的日期各不相同。

  • 0:22The time change can leave people tired and perhaps unhappy the next day.

    时间变化可能会让人第二天感到疲倦，甚至可能心情不佳。

  • 0:31But it also might even harm health.

    但它甚至可能对健康有害。

  • 0:36Some studies have found an increase in heart attacks and strokes right after the March time change.

    一些研究发现，在3月时间变化后，心脏病发作和中风的数量有所增加。

  • 0:46However, there are ways to ease the effects of the time change, including getting more sunshine.

    不过，有一些方法可以减轻时间变化的影响，包括多晒太阳。

  • 0:57The light helps reset your circadian rhythm for healthful sleep.

    光线有助于重新调整你的昼夜节律，实现健康睡眠。

  • 1:05In the U.S., daylight saving time begins Sunday at 2:00 in the morning.

    在美国，夏令时于周日凌晨2:00开始。

  • 1:13The time change will reverse on November 2 when clocks "fall back" as daylight saving time ends.

    时间变化将于11月2日逆转，届时夏令时结束，时钟“回拨”。

  • 1:26The state of Hawaii and most of the state of Arizona do not make the spring change.

    夏威夷州和亚利桑那州的大部分地区不实行春季时间调整。

  • 1:34Those areas remain on standard time along with Puerto Rico, American Samoa, Guam and the U.S. Virgin Islands.

    这些地区与波多黎各、美属萨摩亚、关岛和美属维尔京群岛一样，保持标准时间。

  • 1:48Some people try to prepare for the change to daylight saving time by going to bed a little earlier two or three nights ahead.

    有些人试图通过提前两三天晚上早睡来为夏令时变化做准备。

  • 2:02But with a third of American adults already not getting the suggested seven hours of nightly sleep, catching up can be difficult.

    但由于已有三分之一的美国成年人无法达到建议的每晚七小时睡眠，补觉可能很困难。

  • 2:16The brain has a kind of clock that is set by exposure to sunlight and darkness.

    大脑有一种时钟，由暴露在日光和黑暗中设定。

  • 2:25This clock, known as the circadian rhythm, is on a roughly 24-hour cycle.

    这个时钟，即昼夜节律，大约以24小时为一个周期。

  • 2:35It governs when we become sleepy and when we are more wakeful.

    它控制着我们何时感到困倦，何时更加清醒。

  • 2:42The rhythms change with age.

    节律会随着年龄变化。

  • 2:46This is one reason that early-to-rise young children turn into hard-to-wake teenagers.

    这就是为什么早睡早起的小孩会变成难叫醒的青少年原因之一。

  • 2:57Morning light resets the rhythm.

    晨光可以重置节律。

  • 3:01By evening, levels of a hormone called melatonin begin to increase, leading to tiredness.

    到了晚上，一种叫做褪黑素的激素水平开始上升，导致困倦。

  • 3:11Too much light in the evening - that extra hour from daylight saving time - delays the melatonin increase.

    晚上光线过多——夏令时多出的那一小时——会延迟褪黑素的增加。

  • 3:22As a result, the cycle gets delayed.

    结果，周期被推迟了。

  • 3:26The circadian rhythm affects more than sleep.

    昼夜节律影响的不只是睡眠。

  • 3:31It also influences heart rate, blood pressure, hormone releases and other systems.

    它还影响心率、血压、激素释放和其他系统。

  • 3:41Sleep deprivation, or lack of sleep, is linked to heart disease, weight conditions, problems with thinking and remembering, and more.

    睡眠不足或缺乏睡眠与心脏病、体重问题、思维和记忆障碍等有关。

  • 3:55Deadly car crash numbers increase the first few days after the spring time change, a study of U.S. traffic deaths says.

    一项对美国交通死亡人数的研究显示，春季时间变化后的头几天，致命车祸数量增加。

  • 4:07The risk of crashes is highest in the morning, it found.

    研究发现，车祸风险在早晨最高。

  • 4:13Researchers suggested sleep deprivation might be responsible.

    研究人员认为睡眠不足可能是原因。

  • 4:20The time change also has a link to the heart.

    时间变化也与心脏有关。

  • 4:25The American Heart Association points to studies that suggest an increase in heart attacks on the Monday after daylight saving time begins, and in strokes for two days afterward.

    美国心脏协会指出，有研究表明，夏令时开始后的星期一心脏病发作增加，之后两天中风增加。

  • 4:43Doctors already know that heart attacks, especially severe ones, are a bit more common on Mondays generally - and in the morning, when blood is more likely to clot.

    医生们已经知道，心脏病发作，尤其是严重的心肌梗死，通常在星期一更常见——而且在早晨，血液更容易凝固。

  • 4:60Researchers do not know why the time change would add to that Monday connection.

    研究人员不知道时间变化为何会加剧这种周一关联。

  • 5:08But it is possible the sudden circadian change influences other issues such as high blood pressure in people already at risk.

    但突然的昼夜节律变化可能会影响其他问题，比如已有风险人群的高血压。

  • 5:21To prepare for daylight saving time, experts offer some advice.

    为了应对夏令时，专家们提供了一些建议。

  • 5:28Slowly move bedtimes about 15 or 20 minutes earlier for several nights before the time change.

    在时间变化前的几个晚上，将就寝时间逐渐提前15或20分钟。

  • 5:39Try to rise earlier the next morning, too.

    第二天早上也要尝试早起。

  • 5:45Go outside for early morning sunshine the first week of daylight saving time.

    夏令时第一周，到户外享受清晨的阳光。

  • 5:53This is another way to help reset your body's clock.

    这是帮助重置身体时钟的另一种方法。

  • 5:60Start daily activities, like dinner or exercise, a little earlier.

    将日常活动，如晚餐或锻炼，稍微提前。

  • 6:08This may help tell your body to start getting used to the new conditions, sleep experts suggest.

    睡眠专家建议，这有助于告诉你的身体开始适应新的条件。

  • 6:17Daytime sleeping, the drug caffeine and light from phones and other electronic devices can make an earlier bedtime even harder.

    白天睡觉、咖啡因以及手机和其他电子设备的光线会使早睡变得更加困难。

  • 6:32Americans have discussed ending daylight saving time.

    美国人一直在讨论取消夏令时。

  • 6:37But so far, no official changes have been made.

    但到目前为止，还没有做出任何正式改变。

  • 6:43Health groups such as the American Medical Association and American Academy of Sleep Medicine believe it is time to end time changes.

    美国医学协会和美国睡眠医学学会等健康组织认为，是时候结束时间变更了。

  • 6:57The groups suggest that staying with standard time year-round works better for human biology and sleep needs.

    这些组织建议，全年保持标准时间更符合人类生物学和睡眠需求。

    我是凯蒂·韦弗。
